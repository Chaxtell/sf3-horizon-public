# Hook requests

Horizon is read-only for this project. Each entry: mod, behaviour, hook wanted, workaround in place. Mods stay at data level for the blocked part until the hook exists (`MASS_PORT_PLAN.md` Phase 5).

1. **Crafting remainder with a changed stack** (Mystical Agriculture Infusion Crystal): behaviour `Item.getContainerItem(stack)` returns the same stack with one more damage, destroyed past its durability. Hook wanted: `native_crafting_remainder(player: u128, recipe: &str, ingredient: &ItemStack, remainder: &mut Option<ItemStack>)`, HEAD, out-parameter, called for each ingredient that leaves one (crafting table, inventory grid). `native_item_crafted` only hears the result and the grid's contents are not reachable. Workaround in place: `crafting_remainder` is the crystal itself (no wear).

2. **Custom thrown projectile for a mod item**: Resolved 2026-11-18: 16 content `entity_type` files (`tools/gen_chicken_eggs.py`, snowball template, `item` = the egg) throw the egg as the engine's thrown item; `native_projectile_impact` (+ `native_hears_projectile_impacts`) rolls the jar's hatch where it lands and drops the breed's chicken item there. Spawn eggs are placed, not thrown, in the jar (`ItemSpawnEgg.onItemUse`), so they stay. Left: the chick is the chicken item, not a baby mob, and a dispenser does not throw it (engine: not yet). Numbers, tests, in-game steps: `docs/registry_drafts/hr_2_6_7.md`. (Original, kept: (Chickens colored eggs and spawn eggs, Batch 2C): behaviour: the thrown egg flies and hatches a chick (the breed of its color) where it lands. Hook wanted: spawn an egg-like projectile carrying an item id and get a callback on impact with the hit position. Workaround in place: the egg resolves at the click (1 in 8, then 1 in 32 for 4) and gives the chicken item, not a baby mob.)

3. **Furnace fuel value for a content item**: Resolved 2026-11-18: content `minecraft:cooking_fuel` component (items and block items both take `components`), `tools/gen_fuels.py`. Table and in-game steps: `docs/registry_drafts/hr_3_fuels.md`. (Original ask, kept: (Tiny Progressions Charcoal Block 16000 ticks, Mystical Agriculture Essence Coals, Batch 2H): content items have no fuel field and `native_furnace_burn` only reports a burn. Hook wanted: `native_furnace_fuel_time(item, &mut ticks)` (HEAD, out-parameter) or a content `fuel` property. )

4. **Transient attribute modifier on a player** (Mystical Agriculture Supremium set sprint/air speed, Batch 2H): `native_attribute_modifiers` only lists. Hook wanted: add/remove a named modifier on a player at run time. Workaround: none (per-piece static `minecraft:attribute_modifiers` would apply without the full set).

Hook wanted: a larger menu, or a paged window onto a bigger container with a button hook that can repage. Workaround in place: the Large Crate holds 256.

Numbers, tests, in-game steps: `docs/registry_drafts/hr_2_6_7.md`. (Original, kept: (Pam's HarvestCraft, 18 fish at weight 25 and Seaweed at 10, Batch 2J): `native_player_fish` cannot change the catch. Hook wanted: `native_fishing_loot(player, table, drops)`, or `native_loot_drops` also called for a reeled-in catch. Workaround: none (a full override of vanilla's `gameplay/fishing/*` tables is possible but would replace vanilla's entries).)

10. **Entities in an explosion** (Actually Additions Shock Suppressor, Batch 2K): `native_explosion_detonate` lists blocks only. The detonate hook spares them (squared distance 25, 300 FE each, after the blocks, as the jar) and notes their ids; `native_explosion_knockback` (API 374) zeroes their push and `native_living_incoming_damage_v3` cancels `explosion`/`player_explosion` damage on them (the host too). Approximation: the blast radius is not in the hook, so the entities charged are those within twice the farthest broken block (8 with none). Draft: docs/registry_drafts/hr_9_10_12_35.md. Played 2026-11-18: the knockback part works (a spared player is not pushed), but blast damage does not reach `native_living_incoming_damage` / `_v3`: `explosion.rs` hurts mobs through `hurt_mob` -> `Mob::damage` directly (and the player likewise), so spared entities still take damage. Wanted: the blast's entity damage dispatched through the incoming-damage hooks.

12. **Read and give another block's redstone output** (Actually Additions Phantom Redstoneface, Batch 2M): a helper for `getSignal`/`getDirectSignal` of the block at `pos` on a face (`native_neighbor_signal` is the signal received), and a way for a mod block to emit it (`native_block_signal` is read-only). Wanted: the host's client told of flight changes, and a client hook where the window's player can be pushed. Re-tested 2026-11-18 in play: a full Supremium set on the single-player host (armor tick verified running: Stone Helmet gave Slowness) left `abilities.mayfly` 0b. Still open. Resolved 2026-11-19 (in play, LAN closed): the host flies with a jetpack or an Angel Ring and a Travel Staff blink moves them; the integrated server treats the host as one of its players, and the 'mayfly 0b' seen before was `/data get` reading a stale record. Earlier note: a push of the host does work from `native_client_tick_post` when the server code queues it (bundle 16 vector plates, bundle 10 Air Sigil, played); flight and teleport of the host stay open. Guests: flight and teleport work (played 2026-11-18/19), but `native_entity_set_velocity` refuses players, so no mod can launch a guest.
